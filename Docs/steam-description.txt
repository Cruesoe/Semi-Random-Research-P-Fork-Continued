[b]Research picks from a small, changing set of offered projects instead of the whole tree.[/b]

Instead of choosing freely from the entire research tree, you choose from a few offered projects and adapt your colony's plans as new options appear. Designed especially for large mod lists and tech-progression playthroughs.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A small set of research projects is offered at a time, rerolled when a project is finished.
[*]Anomaly dark research and Vanilla Gravship Expanded gravtech research get their own offers alongside standard research.
[*]Right-click an offer to snooze it: it is not offered again until you unsnooze it, and its place stays empty until the next reroll.
[*]Snoozing a project also sets aside the research that depends on it, after a confirmation listing every project affected, and the snoozed list shows what is set aside.
[*]Auto research starts the cheapest offer for you when the research tab closes with nothing being researched.
[*]Research can be paused and resumed from the research tab.
[*]Every offer shows how long it will take at your colony's research speed.
[*]A timings panel under the current research shows your research speed, the time left on the current project and the time to finish all remaining research or your current tech level, with an optional rate graph.
[*]A progress bar shows completed research for each tech level, with your faction's and the world's tech level.
[*]Each project lists what it unlocks, its unfinished prerequisites, and the unfinished projects that require it.
[*]A history view lists recently completed research and what each project unlocked.
[*]Offers can be grouped and colored by tech level.
[*]The window can grow to show every offer or snoozed project instead of using a scroll bar.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Number of choices[/b]: a constant number, or more choices as the colony grows.
[*][b]Rerolls[/b]: reroll every choice when a project is finished, and allow manual rerolls never, once per project or always.
[*][b]Which projects are offered[/b]: restrict offers to your faction's tech level, finish the lowest tech level first, allow one higher-tech project, and balance the cost of the offers.
[*][b]Partially researched projects[/b]: offer them normally, never, as a replacement choice or as an extra choice.
[*][b]Display[/b]: the rate graph, the completion letter, opening the research tab on completion, and adaptive window size can each be turned off, estimates can show the in-game date they finish instead of the time left, and the timings panel can time your current tech level instead of all research.
[*][b]Snoozing[/b]: allow snoozing, and ask before snoozing a project other research depends on.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with Node Research, YART, Sleek Research Tab, Nice Research Tab and Research: Organized, with a button in each to switch back. Also supports Progression: Core, Genesis, Vanilla Factions Expanded - Tribals, Vanilla Gravship Expanded and Save Our Ship 2.
Not compatible with Research: Auto or Research Queue, because they choose your research for you.
Remove any other version of Semi Random Research before enabling this one.
Safe to add to an existing save.
