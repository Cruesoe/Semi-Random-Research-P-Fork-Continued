[h1]Make research less predictable[/h1]
Instead of choosing freely from the entire research tree, select from a smaller set of offered projects and adapt your colony’s plans as new options appear.

Designed especially for large modlists and tech-progression playthroughs.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Choose how many research projects are offered.
[*]Configure automatic rerolling.
[*]Snooze unwanted projects or switch between available choices.
[*]Automatically select the next research project.
[*]Restrict research to your faction’s tech level.
[*]Scale the number of choices with your colonist count.
[*]Balance offered projects by research cost.
[*]See completion estimates, research speed, averages, and progress history.
[*]Track completed research across each tech level.
[*]View faction and world tech levels when supported.
[*]See what buildings and items each project unlocks.
[*]Optionally group projects by tech level.
[*]Hide the research graph and disable research-complete letters or automatic window opening.
[/list]

Includes support for Anomaly dark research and Vanilla Gravship Expanded.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Compatible with:
[list]
[*]Yet Another Research Tree
[*]Sleek Research Tab
[*]Node Research
[*]Progression: Core
[*]World Tech Level
[/list]

[b]Not compatible with Research: Auto.[/b]

[h2]FAQ[/h2]
[b]Is it safe to add to an existing save?[/b]
Yes.

[b]Does this replace older versions of Semi Random Research?[/b]
Yes. Remove other versions of Semi Random Research from your mod list before enabling this one.
